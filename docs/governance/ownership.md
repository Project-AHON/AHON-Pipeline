# Ownership

Who owns which area of the pipeline. The owner is the person who answers questions about the area and reviews decisions that affect it. The backup covers when the owner is away.

This page does not decide who reviews pull requests. Reviewers are assigned automatically, as described in [CONTRIBUTING.md](../../CONTRIBUTING.md).
